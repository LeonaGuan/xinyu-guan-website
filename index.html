Xinyu Guan

Ph.D. Candidate in Educational Leadership & Policy Analysis

University of Wisconsin-Madison

I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Educational Leadership and Policy Analysis at UW-Madison, advised by Dr. Christopher M. Saldaña. My research sits at the intersection of K-12 school finance and educational equity, with a focus on how funding policies shape opportunities for multilingual learners. I bring an interdisciplinary perspective informed by prior work in finance, auditing, and English language teaching across Canada, China, Thailand and the United States.

Research Interests

K-12 School Finance

Examining the policy, practice, and politics of how schools are funded, with particular attention to equity in resource distribution.

Bilingual Education Policy

Investigating funding models and program effectiveness for bilingual and bicultural learners in state education systems.

Quantitative Methods

Applying causal inference, quasi-experimental design, and advanced survey methodology to education policy questions.
