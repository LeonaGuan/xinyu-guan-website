Research

My research examines the intersection of K-12 school finance policy and educational equity, with particular attention to bilingual education funding and how policy design affects resource distribution for historically underserved learners.

Research Interests

I study how education funding policies are designed, implemented, and experienced across different state contexts. My work draws on different kinds of quantitative methods and critical policy analysis to ask how public resources reach — or fail to reach — the students who need them most. I am particularly interested in how states fund bilingual and bicultural education programs, and in the broader politics and practice of K-12 school finance reform.

Open Research Tools

Bilingual Education Policy Tracker

Open Access

An interactive dashboard I built that maps and catalogs bilingual education policies across all 50 U.S. states, incorporating funding data from district, state, and federal levels. The tool allows researchers, policymakers, and educators to explore how states classify bilingual education — from mandated programs to restrictive policies — and to compare legislative approaches and funding patterns across regions and over time.
