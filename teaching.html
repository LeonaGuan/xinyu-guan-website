Teaching

I am passionate about making quantitative methods accessible and empowering students to use data for meaningful inquiry.

Experience

Data Science Workshop

Graduate Teaching Assistant, University of Wisconsin–Madison 2023–2025

Designed and facilitated a hands-on data science workshop for the Wisconsin Idea Executive Ph.D. cohort of mid-career education practitioners. The workshop introduced these doctoral students to computational tools and data analysis techniques for education research, bridging the gap between applied leadership experience and quantitative inquiry.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe that effective teaching begins with meeting students where they are. Whether introducing doctoral students to their first line of code or helping English learners build confidence in a new language, my approach centers on creating inclusive, supportive learning environments where curiosity drives discovery.
